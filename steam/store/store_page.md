## Short description (236 of 300 characters)

Drive a tiny RC truck across deformable desert sand. Haul fragile cargo through four hostile rings, bolt parts onto your rig in the workshop, and outwit beetles, hawks and scorpions that all want what you are carrying.

## About this game

Sand Rover is a single-player physics delivery game about a very small truck in a very large desert. The sand deforms under your wheels: you dig ruts, bog down on soft dunes, and learn to read the ground. Every contract is a load to get from A to B in one piece, and the desert has opinions about that.

A story of 28 contracts takes you through four rings: the Home Dunes, the Salt Flats, the Glass Desert, and the low-gravity Drift. Beetles ram you, ants carry off dropped cargo, a hawk snatches anything you did not strap down, scorpions clamp your wheels and drift rays flip you over. You answer with a winch, a spare wheel, a rescue tow, and three outposts you build to open up fast travel.

Between runs, take the truck into the workshop and bolt parts onto 19 sockets around the chassis. Save up to three builds and swap them to suit the job. Full controller support is included.
